# Governance

Vyasa is led by its creator, techgopal ([@techgopal](https://github.com/techgopal)),
as lead maintainer, who has final say on direction and releases.

**Maintainers** review and merge pull requests in the areas they own
(`.github/CODEOWNERS`). A contributor who has landed sustained, high-quality
work in an area may be invited to become a maintainer of it; the lead
maintainer and existing maintainers decide together.

**Decisions** are made in the open, in issues and Discussions. Large changes
(new subsystems, breaking API changes) start as a Discussion with a short
proposal before code.

This document will grow as the project does.
